NABI Lab · Yoonsung Nam

Nanoscale Bioengineering Laboratory at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology

Our group is dedicated to designing and fabricating functional nanostructures through biomolecular self-assembly. We focus on applications in developing vaccines and therapeutics to combat infectious diseases and cancer. We explore a variety of biochemical recognition and selection mechanisms across biological systems.

Research

Biomolecular self-assembly for vaccines and therapeutics.

01

Multilayered lipid vesicles for nanovaccines

We develop multilayered lipid vesicles to improve carrier stability and the endosomal escape of antigens. Our biomimetic approach aims to optimize these structures for vaccine delivery.

02

Self-assembled nanoparticles for drug and gene delivery

We engineer self-assembled nanocarriers for siRNA and anti-cancer drugs. Our work spans multivalent cell targeting, peptide ligands, light-controlled drug release, and serum albumin-based nanoparticles.
